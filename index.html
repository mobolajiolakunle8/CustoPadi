Install & share

Put CustoPadi on your phone screen, or send it to other business owners.

Put CustoPadi on your phone screen

On Android (Chrome): Tap the 3 dots ⋮ at the top right, then tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button at the bottom, then tap Add to Home Screen.

Share CustoPadi with other businesses

Send the link on WhatsApp so other shop owners can register their own business.

For the app owner: icon files missing

Phones need icon-192.png and icon-512.png next to index.html to show the CustoPadi icon. Download them, put them in your app folder and publish again.

Forgot your PIN?

For your safety, nobody can see your PIN, not even CustoPadi. But we can help you get back in.

  1. 1Message CustoPadi support on WhatsApp.
  2. 2We confirm it's really you and reset your login.
  3. 3Tap Create account with the same number and choose a new PIN. All your customers will still be there.
