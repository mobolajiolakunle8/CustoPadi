About your data

Live accounts use Firebase Authentication and Firestore. The owner must publish owner-only database rules. CustoPadi also keeps a recovery copy in this browser. Put CustoPadi on your phone screen

On Android (Chrome): Tap the 3 dots ⋮ at the top right, then tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).

On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button at the bottom, then tap Add to Home Screen.

How to put CustoPadi online for free

To install on phones, put these files together in one folder:

  • index.html
  • manifest.webmanifest
  • sw.js
  • icon.svg
  • icon-192.png (download below)
  • icon-512.png (download below)
  • logo.png (optional, your real logo)

Then put the folder online:

  1. Netlify Drop (easiest): go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder onto the page. You get a live https://...netlify.app link straight away.
  2. Vercel or Cloudflare Pages: upload the folder at vercel.com or pages.cloudflare.com.
  3. GitHub Pages: upload the files to a GitHub repository and turn on Settings → Pages.

Open the new https:// link on your phone in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then come back to this screen.
